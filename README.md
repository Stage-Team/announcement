# Announcements
**This repo is intended for releasing announcements**, such as updates, releases, important things. and more.

## The Stage Team Manifesto

We are the Stage Team.
We are not here to imitate others.
We are not here to wait for someone to give us permission.

We are here to build.

We believe that age is not a limitation.
We believe that tools should be free.
We believe that anyone, anywhere in the world, has the right to build, to learn, and to own their work.

We are building a complete ecosystem.
From the lowest layer to the highest.
From what makes the hardware come alive,
to what lets you create.

We believe that:
- Code is our common language.
- Freedom is our right.
- Building is our responsibility.
- And persistence is our weapon.

We are not afraid of:
- Being taken lightly.
- Being told "you are too young."
- Being told "this can't be done."
- Being laughed at.

Because we know:
Those who changed the world,
were always laughed at first.
Then ignored.
Then fought.
And in the end, they won.

We are the Stage Team.
And this is just the beginning.
